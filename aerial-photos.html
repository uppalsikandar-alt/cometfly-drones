Aerial Stock Photos & Footage

Searching for aerial photos or video for your next project?

License high-resolution drone imagery of Sydney sites, structures and landscapes direct from the pilots who flew it — ready for websites, social media, real estate, reports, tenders, film and editorial use.

Why buy direct

No stock-site markups. No middleman.

Big stock libraries keep a large share of every sale and pay the photographer a small commission. When you licence from CometFly Drones, you deal straight with the licensed operator who captured the image — so pricing stays fair and questions get answered by the person who flew it.

Every image is captured under CASA RePL licensed operations, and full-resolution originals are supplied without watermarks once your licence is confirmed.
