Process

How a mission runs.

  1. Brief & site assessment

    We confirm the asset, objective and airspace requirements, then plan the flight.

  2. Flight & data capture

    CASA RePL licensed pilots fly the mission and capture imagery or survey data on site.

  3. Processing

    Imagery is processed into inspection reports, orthomosaics, 3D models or volumetric data.

  4. Delivery

    Findings are delivered in a clear, usable format for your team, engineers or clients.
