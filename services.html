Detailed aerial imagery to identify visible damage, deterioration and maintenance issues — without unnecessary roof access.

Building inspections

Aerial visual inspections of façades, roofs and other hard-to-access areas, supporting condition checks and maintenance planning.

Infrastructure inspections
