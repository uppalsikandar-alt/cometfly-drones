About Us

Safe, efficient and reliable aerial data.

We combine advanced drone technology with careful planning and attention to detail to deliver safe, efficient and reliable aerial data that helps our clients make informed decisions.

Every operation is planned around the specific requirements of the site, including airspace, access, environmental conditions and safety considerations. Our operations are conducted in accordance with applicable CASA requirements and relevant aviation regulations.

From detailed aerial inspections to mapping and photogrammetry, CometFly Drones is focused on delivering accurate, practical and professional solutions for every project.
